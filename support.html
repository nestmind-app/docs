iCloud Sync

Save on your iPhone. See it on your iPad. Everything stays in sync automatically.

What You Need

  • Same Apple ID on all devices
  • iCloud Drive enabled
  • NestMind granted iCloud access

How It Works

Once enabled, your library syncs through Apple's iCloud. Changes appear on all your devices automatically. No buttons to press, nothing to configure.

🔒
Privacy Note
Sync goes directly through Apple. We never see your data. It's encrypted end-to-end.

Tips & Troubleshooting

First-Time Sync Taking a While?

If you have a large library, the initial sync needs the app to stay open. Grab a coffee. This only happens once.

Reinstalled the App?

Welcome back! Head to Settings and re-enable iCloud sync to restore your library from the cloud.

Sync Seems Stuck?

Try closing and reopening the app, or check your internet connection. Usually does the trick.

💡
Pro Tip
Keep NestMind in the foreground during large syncs for the fastest results.

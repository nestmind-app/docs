Our Vision

The Origin

NestMind started with a simple frustration: scattered knowledge.

YouTube videos you meant to watch again. Bookmarks buried in folders you forgot existed. Notes scattered across apps. Podcasts with insights you'll never find twice. Your knowledge lives in a dozen different places, and none of them talk to each other.

But that's only half the problem.

The apps that promise to organize your life have a hidden cost: you become the product. They track what you save, analyze what you search, and profile what you read. Then they use it to train AI models and target ads. Read the fine print. Your content isn't yours. And good luck truly deleting anything.

We built NestMind because we wanted something different. Something that actually helps you find what you've saved. Something that understands meaning, not just keywords. Something that respects your privacy absolutely.

— The NestMind Team

Core Philosophy

Four principles guide everything we build.

🔒
01

Privacy is non-negotiable

Your knowledge is personal. It reveals what you're learning, what you're curious about, what matters to you. This should never leave your device without your explicit choice. All AI processing happens locally. No exceptions.

🔍
02

Search by meaning, not memory

Keyword search is broken. You save an article about "improving focus" but search for "concentration tips" and find nothing. Semantic search understands intent. Describe what you're looking for in your own words and NestMind finds it.

🏠
03

One home for everything

Context-switching kills productivity. Jumping between YouTube, Safari, Notes, and bookmarks wastes time and mental energy. Your knowledge deserves a single, unified home where everything connects and nothing gets lost.

✨
04

AI that works for you

Most AI extracts value from you. We believe AI should amplify your capabilities without compromising your privacy. Apple Intelligence on your device means AI that never phones home. Your knowledge stays yours.

The Future

We're building toward a world where your second brain is truly yours.

Imagine asking "What was that productivity technique from the podcast last month?" and getting an instant, accurate answer. Imagine your knowledge base understanding connections you never explicitly made. Imagine an AI assistant that knows everything you've ever saved, running entirely on your device.

This is what NestMind is becoming. Not a cloud service that holds your data hostage. Not an AI that learns from your content to serve others. A genuine personal knowledge companion that exists to serve you and only you.

Our Commitments

Native first

Built with SwiftUI and iOS 26. No cross-platform compromises. NestMind feels like it belongs on your device because it was designed for nothing else.

On-device AI

Apple Intelligence powers semantic search and the AI assistant. Everything runs locally. Your data never leaves to train models or improve services.

Sync without surveillance

iCloud sync keeps your devices in harmony. Apple's encryption protects your data in transit and at rest. We never see your content.

Simplicity over features

Every feature earns its place. We'd rather do three things brilliantly than ten things adequately. Complexity is the enemy of daily use.

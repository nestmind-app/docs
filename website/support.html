Getting Started

Adding Content

Add bookmarks or videos to your library:

  • Paste URLs — Copy a link and paste it in NestMind
  • iOS Share Sheet — Share from Safari or any app directly to NestMind

Organizing with Threads

"Threads" enable grouping of todos, bookmarks, and videos into unified project contexts. Use them to organize related content together.

💡
Pro Tip
Use semantic search to find content by meaning, not just keywords. Try searching for concepts like "iOS animations" instead of exact titles.

iCloud Sync (CloudKit)

Your data syncs automatically across all your Apple devices.

Requirements

  • Same Apple ID on all devices
  • iCloud Drive enabled
  • NestMind permissions granted for iCloud

How It Works

When CloudKit is enabled, existing local data uploads to iCloud. Changes sync automatically in the background.

⚠️
Note
Large libraries may require the app to stay in the foreground during initial sync.

AI Assistant

NestMind's AI Assistant helps you search and interact with your knowledge base.

Privacy

  • Operates entirely on-device
  • No data sent to external servers
  • Uses Apple's Foundation Models framework
🔒
Future Features
Any future network-dependent features will require explicit user consent before any data transmission.

Data & Privacy

You have full control over your data.

Export & Delete

Access export or deletion options via:

Settings → Data Management

  • Export — Download all your data
  • Delete — Remove data from the app
  • Clear iCloud — Manage via iOS Settings → iCloud

For full details, see our Privacy Policy.

Known Issues

Large Library Sync

If you have a large library, initial sync may require the app to stay in the foreground for extended time. This is a one-time process.

Reinstalling the App

After reinstalling, you'll need to re-enable iCloud sync in Settings to restore your data from iCloud.

💡
Tip
If sync seems stuck, try closing and reopening the app, or check your internet connection.

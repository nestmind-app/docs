Privacy Policy

Your data belongs to you — always.

🔒
Effective Date: October 3, 2025
NestMind is built on a simple principle: your data stays yours.

NestMind is designed with privacy at its core. All AI processing happens on your device, and we don't collect, store, or have access to your personal information.

Data Storage

NestMind uses iCloud through Apple's CloudKit for secure storage and sync.

  • iCloud Sync — Your data syncs securely through Apple's infrastructure
  • Local Caching — Enables offline access with iOS encryption protection
  • Your Apple ID — Data is tied to your Apple ID, not to us
💡
Key Point
All synchronization occurs directly through Apple's iCloud infrastructure. We never see your data.

What We Don't Collect

We explicitly avoid gathering:

  • ❌ Personal identifiers
  • ❌ Advertising IDs
  • ❌ Location data
  • ❌ Workspace content
  • ❌ Usage analytics
  • ❌ Search queries
  • ❌ Browsing behavior
✋
No Tracking
We don't track you. Period.

Data Processing

All operations occur locally on your device:

  • Saving — Content is stored locally and synced to your iCloud
  • Searching — Semantic search runs entirely on-device
  • Organizing — Tags, folders, and organization stay local
  • AI Assistant — Uses Apple's on-device Foundation Models

No third-party services receive your data. No external API calls are made with your content.

Permissions

Clipboard Access

Clipboard access activates only during voluntary link-pasting. We don't monitor or store clipboard history.

Microphone (Optional)

If you use voice input, speech-to-text processes locally without audio retention. No recordings are stored or transmitted.

Your Controls

You have complete control over your data:

  • Disable iCloud Sync — Keep data local only
  • Export Data — Download everything you've saved
  • Delete Data — Remove information directly in the app
  • Remove Synced Content — Manage via iCloud settings

Access these options in Settings → Data Management within the app.

Security

Local Data

Local data receives iOS-level encryption, protected by your device passcode and Secure Enclave.

iCloud Data

iCloud data relies on Apple's security infrastructure, encrypted in transit and at rest, tied exclusively to your Apple ID.

Children's Privacy

NestMind doesn't knowingly collect information from users under 13. All data is user-initiated — we don't create profiles or collect data automatically.
